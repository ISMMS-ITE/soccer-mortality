# soccer-mortality
Update mortality in a cohort of professional soccer players

Original cohort study: Taioli E. All causes of mortality in male professional soccer players. Eur J Public Health. 2007 Dec;17(6):600-4. doi: 10.1093/eurpub/ckm035. Epub 2007 Apr 12. PMID: 17434875.

Updated vital status of Italian professional soccer players through 31 December, 2023 to examine overall and cause-specific mortality and compare to initial findings in the same cohort from 2007.
